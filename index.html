Cosmic History: August 10, 1998 🌠

The Perseid Meteor Shower Peaks

As you drew your very first breath on Earth, our planet was slamming into the dense, icy debris stream of Comet Swift-Tuttle. This caused the sky to explode with hundreds of brilliant shooting stars per hour, welcoming you with a natural cosmic firework show.

🪐

The Lunar Occultation & Conjunction of Jupiter

On the exact night of August 10, 1998, the solar system aligned beautifully. The Waning Gibbous Moon passed incredibly close to Jupiter in our night sky, causing the giant planet to gleam with intense radiance right alongside the moon's surface.

🚀

NASA's Cassini Speeds Past Earth

While you were settling into your crib, humanity's most ambitious deep-space probe, Cassini, was executing deep space trajectory loops right in Earth's cosmic neighborhood, gathering blinding momentum to complete its multi-year voyage out to the ringed planet Saturn.
